RED
High Difficulty / Technical DemandAll-barre songs, fast tempo, drop D, or technique requiring special attention. The songs that need the most Phase 2/3 work.
AMBER
Feel / Pattern TagsDominant rhythmic approach or arrangement character. Your strum library maps directly to these.
CYAN
Technique / Voicing NotesSpecific left or right-hand technique markers. What the Phase 2 toolkit was built to handle.
GREEN
Opportunities / AccessibilityGood embellishment moments, open voicings, or arrangements that reward nuance over brute force.
PURPLE
Performance CharacterEmotional feel and dynamic control matter more than technical difficulty here. The challenge is invisible technique.
